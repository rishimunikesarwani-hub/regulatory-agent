# VS3 language and meaning review

Date: 2026-10-11.
Reference: ASD-STE100 Issue 9, dated 2025-01-15.

## Scope and protected content

The review covers the new slice specification, development guide, evidence record, and changed project documentation.
The existing approved specifications remain the requirements source.
The changes keep API routes, source identifiers, dates, model configuration, budget limits, ports, branches, and private database paths.
They keep local acceptance different from AWS acceptance.
They keep controlled responses different from live model results.

The project glossary defines API, applicability, citation, fixture, idempotency key, job, snapshot, stage trace, and worktree.
Required product labels and machine identifiers keep their exact spelling.

## Reviews completed

The mechanical checker examined sentence lengths, paragraph lengths, contractions, and semicolons.
Two long paragraphs in the first specification check were divided by topic.
The corrected specification and guide have no mechanical findings.
The changed documentation extract was checked separately from unchanged historical prose.

The manual review checked instructions, conditions, limits, actors, negative requirements, and meaning.
Instruction sentences use at most 20 words.
Descriptive sentences use at most 25 words.
The review kept one instruction per sentence and consistent technical terms.

The meaning review kept the historical date and fixture restrictions unchanged.
It kept missing operational evidence, unverified applicability, and current-source failures explicit.
It kept review revisions, cutoff rules, immutable originals, and failed-save recovery requirements.
It introduced no live quality or deployment claim.

## Limits

The full approved-meaning dictionary review remains incomplete.
The checker covers selected structural rules only.
These results do not establish full ASD-STE100 compliance or ASD approval.
